Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's exactly how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: March 1, 2025

Summary: DriveNorth Technologies Inc. ("DriveNorth," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it in compliance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws.

1. Who We Are

DriveNorth Technologies Inc. is a Canadian company headquartered in Toronto, Ontario. We operate drivenorth.ca, a consumer-first automotive lead-generation platform that connects Canadians with vehicle dealers and financing partners.

Our Privacy Officer can be reached at: privacy@drivenorth.ca

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Complete a vehicle or financing inquiry form
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Contact us via our contact form or by email/phone
  • Apply as a dealer partner

Information you provide directly:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location information (province, city)
  • Vehicle preferences (type, make, budget range)
  • General credit situation (self-reported category — we do not pull credit reports)
  • Purchase timeline and financing preferences

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type and operating system
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring URLs and search terms used to find our site
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 8)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide our service: Matching you with appropriate Canadian dealer partners and financing providers based on your stated preferences.
  • To communicate with you: Sending confirmation emails, follow-up information, and responses to your inquiries.
  • To improve our platform: Analyzing usage patterns to enhance the user experience and matching accuracy.
  • For marketing (with consent): Sending newsletters, guides, and promotional offers if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • For legal compliance: Meeting our obligations under applicable Canadian laws.

4. Sharing Your Information

We share your personal information only in the following circumstances:

With matched dealer partners:

When you submit a vehicle or financing inquiry, we share your contact information and stated preferences with up to three pre-screened Canadian dealer or lending partners who match your criteria. This sharing only occurs with your explicit consent, which you provide by submitting our form.

With service providers:

We use trusted third-party service providers to operate our platform (e.g., email delivery, analytics, CRM). These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and may only use your information to provide services to us.

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to any third party
  • Share your data with marketing companies unrelated to automotive services
  • Share your information with parties outside Canada without your consent (except as required by law)

5. Credit Information

DriveNorth does not access, pull, or store your credit report at any stage. The credit information you provide on our forms is a self-reported general category (e.g., "Good," "Fair," "Poor") used solely for matching purposes. Any formal credit inquiry is conducted only by a lender you choose to proceed with, and only with your direct consent.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Typically:

  • Lead/inquiry data: Up to 24 months from the date of submission
  • Email subscription data: Until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics data: Up to 26 months (aggregated and anonymized where possible)

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at privacy@drivenorth.ca.

7. Your Rights Under PIPEDA

As a Canadian resident, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw your consent to our use of your information (subject to legal and contractual restrictions)
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information
  • Complaint: File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC) at www.priv.gc.ca

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@drivenorth.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve our website. Cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site functionality (e.g., form sessions). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site (e.g., Google Analytics). You can opt out via your browser settings.
  • Marketing cookies: Used to show relevant advertising on other websites. You can opt out via the Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada at youradchoices.ca.

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage for personal data at rest
  • Access controls limiting who can view personal data internally
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing

While we take these precautions, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and the OPC as required by law.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., dealer partner sites). This Privacy Policy applies only to drivenorth.ca. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

DriveNorth is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or a notice on our website.

13. Contact Us

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding your personal information, please contact:

  • Privacy Officer, DriveNorth Technologies Inc.
  • 100 King Street West, Suite 5600, Toronto, ON M5X 1C9
  • Email: privacy@drivenorth.ca
  • Phone: 1-800-555-1234

This Privacy Policy was last reviewed and updated on March 1, 2025. DriveNorth Technologies Inc. is committed to PIPEDA compliance and the responsible handling of all personal information.
